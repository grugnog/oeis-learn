# Bounded non-LODA measurement and decision contract

Normative for FR-024–038. These are implementation-time protocols and planning defaults, not measurements already made or instructions to start training during this documentation task. Known correctness repairs are mandatory regardless of measured learning benefit. Only LODA's runtime, paired language comparison and conditional transcoder are outside 007.

## One decision ledger; no Cartesian sweep

Each `ExperimentDecision` identifies the question, hypothesis, baseline/challenger profile hashes, code/runtime/data identities, seeds, fixed budgets, primary metric, minimum useful effect, correctness gates, selection rule, stop rule and final disposition. Status is `adopted`, `baseline_retained`, `not_triggered`, `inconclusive` or `failed_gate`. A not-triggered decision records the actual diagnostic and threshold that was not met; it cannot close a known bug-repair task. Inconclusive never expands a budget automatically. Synthetic program families and OEIS groups are reported separately; treat neither multiple implementations nor correlated terms as independent samples. No final-test information is used.

Sequence:

1. Complete G1–G7 correctness, packaging, policy/provenance and resume checks needed by the measured modes. Unit fixtures and standalone GPU feasibility can run before the full release; architectural/learning comparison must not precede known solver/proof repairs.
2. Run one consolidated engineering session, at most **two hours** of active device/CPU measurement, with fixed 128 generic prompts, seeds 7001–7003 and immutable program/model fixtures. Warm up for 10 iterations and collect 30 paired measurements; alternate variant order. Synchronize device boundaries. Profile 3 representative updates. Record inference-only and end-to-end phases separately. Smaller fixture completion is not a substitute for the declared sample size; report shortfall/inconclusive.
3. Reserve **30 minutes** for one frozen-baseline diagnostic (bootstrap/updates/development/trigger collection), then run at most **one baseline/challenger training comparison**, with at most **six hours total** including that diagnostic, bootstrap, updates, development evaluation and checkpointing. Each paired arm has **55 minutes**: first pair seed7001; only if its predeclared useful-effect rule is met may the remaining220 minutes run55-minute arms for seeds7002 and7003. Start paired arms from matched random-initialization rules or the same permitted parent checkpoint; declare which question is being tested. Never compare fresh initialization against a warm-start without naming that confound. Separate best checkpoints by a common development selection schedule at15/30/45/55 minutes. At the hard cap use the last completed scheduled evaluation; do not refund failed or interrupted work.
4. Record each other architecture suggestion as not_triggered only when measured conditions fail; use baseline_retained with a selected-higher-priority reason for a triggered alternative, and inconclusive when required diagnostics are absent. Its bounded implementation/interface tests still execute where required. No additional multi-day arms launch. A failed gate yields focused repairs and affected tests, not another optimization sweep.

The30-minute diagnostic stops at its cap or1000 completed updates, whichever first; it does not promise enough evidence for every trigger. An immutable earlier qualifying007 diagnostic may supply additional measurements only with matching data/model/provenance identities and separately reported historical compute. Missing evidence yields inconclusive, never extra unbudgeted training or false not_triggered.

These eight hours are an upper cap, not a mandatory spend or a user-confirmed optimal duration. The user's 1–3 days **per language** applies to the deferred LODA/WAT comparison. A zero-yield bootstrap or absent GPU can stop 007's measurement path with a failed gate; it cannot qualify the feature by manufacturing examples or using CPU.

## Selecting the single training contrast

Freeze diagnostic thresholds and selection order in `configs/foundation/experiments.yaml` before inspecting comparative scores. Default order:

1. Exact-byte versus tri-stream conditioning if exact source values collide at the old feature boundary on the declared adjacent-integer diagnostic. Both paths are implemented and tested; exact serialization is required regardless of which model learns better.
2. SFT versus verified self-training if at least 100 new provenance-valid, structurally distinct training examples are found within the fixed search diagnostic, and plain SFT has plateaued (less than 1% relative validation-loss improvement across three equal 100-update windows).
3. SFT/self-training versus repaired GRPO if valid candidate fraction is >=25%, at least 25% of groups have nonzero reward variance, and the same plateau condition holds. REINFORCE is implemented as the analytic debugging control, not an automatic third arm.
4. Search/macro/value-guided repair if >=50% of bounded generated attempts are duplicate sources or prefix-matching failures and the permitted archive contains >=100 eligible programs. Compare sampling against one declared portfolio at equal total budgets; learned proposer training uses only generic/training data.
5. Cached sinusoidal versus RoPE if long-body synthetic validation accuracy is at least 20 percentage points below the short-body bucket after matched structural split and coverage checks. A prefix-derived ancestor-path encoding is implemented as a selectable structural variant with unique parent paths; it is chosen instead of RoPE only if a fixed binding/scope diagnostic identifies that cause. Never use future target AST structure.
6. Small model versus 25M-class model if train loss remains >0.5 nats/token and validation loss within 10% of train loss after 1000 updates on >=10,000 verified structurally diverse training programs. A bounded 25M variant is sufficient; do not instantiate every size from 25M–60M.

The first triggered condition selects the one contrast. If no measured condition triggers, retain the baseline and skip paired training; unmeasured conditions remain inconclusive. The initial30-minute diagnostic is still charged. These heuristics organize a small pilot; they do not establish causal diagnoses. Record competing triggers and attribution limits. Encoder, positional and capacity interfaces must be implemented and fixture-tested even when their comparison is not triggered.

Primary learning metric: unique development groups solved within the frozen generation/execution budget per charged arm-hour; also require no reduction in solved-group count at matched compute and report selected top-one, any-of-budget, synthetic structural generalization and failure classes. A useful effect is >=5% relative improvement; if baseline solves zero, require at least two additional groups. Complete the first pair before comparing. Confirmation requires the direction and useful-effect threshold in at least two of three paired seeds and no aggregate solved-group regression. Otherwise retain baseline or mark inconclusive. Three seeds provide limited exploratory evidence, not a universal statistical guarantee; no final claim of broad superiority is warranted from this pilot alone.

## Overflow cost and wider intermediates

Use up to 256 distinct programs in each available stratum: random generic samples, model samples and already verified solutions. Freeze sources, input indices 0–99 and provenance before tracing; do not fill an unavailable stratum with imported solutions. The Python-int reference has a 4096-bit intermediate cap, 50M aggregate steps and the existing external deadline. Its limit means unknown, not unlimited arithmetic. Persist logical-operation signed range violations, maximum intermediate bit length, final output ranges and failure/resource statuses. Native limb wraps are tracked separately and cannot masquerade as mathematical wide overflow.

For programs independently shown entirely in-range, compare checked and unchecked lowering with identical sources and runtime settings: 10 warmups, 30 alternating timed repeats, fuel and synchronized wall time separately. Tracing and timing use separate executions. Unchecked modules are benchmark artifacts and cannot enter strict acceptance or training. If too few programs finish, report the population/shortfall; do not estimate coverage from censored samples.

Where the wider reference completes, count overflowing candidates whose outputs fit signed-256, those that match permitted prefixes, and those that also match sealed development continuations. Report unique added target groups separately from rescued programs. Development verification cannot feed per-target regeneration or replay; final truth remains closed. No statement about the fraction of all OEIS algorithms requiring wider intermediates follows from this sample. Native LODA versus WASM will conflate runtime, primitive and arithmetic choices; 008 must preserve this attribution limit.

## Stop and interpretation rules

Immediately stop the affected run on semantic disagreement, invalid provenance, hidden-data exposure, nonfinite updates, required-evidence quota exhaustion or failed containment. Preserve evidence and use non-success/unknown status; never silently shrink the denominator or restart its clock. Engineering changes can be adopted without a training arm only after their declared equivalence and practical performance gate. All measurement results, including failures and not-triggered decisions, are retained in the release handoff.
