## Boundaries and retry

The campaign advances through diagnostic registration → historical audit and disposition → prospective freeze and production acceptance → pilot → conditional independent replication → publication and verification. An audit can terminate directly at publication. CPU development and mock validation may precede workstation acceptance. Prospective research workers require the accepted runtime and frozen campaign. Only the graph owner can make a conditional task runnable. Record skipped branch nodes and their reasons so progress has a defined denominator.

Within a unit reserve against its logical key and new attempt ordinal, perform the bounded work, write private data and its seal durably, commit the output and charge through the existing journals, then record the verified run boundary. On restart, reconcile reservation, durable data, output commitment and boundary in that order under the owner lock. A valid sealed result left before binding can be adopted after authenticating its exact parents and charge state. A committed result with a missing boundary is rebound idempotently. Unsealed or contradictory data stays as failed evidence. Physical work may repeat where no completed result can be proved. No duplicate scientific credit or free retry is permitted.

Prefer batch boundaries between complete portfolios. A future attempt on a partly executed case inherits the recorded draws, seeds and **provable remaining original** time and token allowances. A vanished process or cross-boot clock can make a residual deadline unknowable. Preserve the unresolved case and its charges in that event. A separately authorized replay gets its own identity, profile and spending record. The training role restores at a valid full-update boundary and records discarded partial work and remaining reservation separately.

## Private replay and public schema

Keep candidate bytes, indexed terms, trained tensors, individual case labels, crosswalks, native traces and exact file paths on the workstation. A share contains the registration and source identities, whole-manifest commitments, counts by scope, anonymous family/component contribution vectors with cross-stratum links, paired seed and arm counts, randomization settings, aggregate charge/timing basis and private verification receipt. Use release-scoped anonymous group labels. Ensure the transport cannot recover private cases from excerpts, embedded archives, compressed bytes or singleton identifiers.

The route projection includes the histogram of feasible diagnosis sets (S, P, neither and their allowed combinations), reproducible fault/replay dispositions, protected denominators and funding/availability flags from the registration. The public verifier reconstructs the routing interval and table precedence from these counts. No case identifier is needed.

The sufficient statistics preserve per-family sizes, joint arm/seed success, measured and unresolved counts, retention and matched gain/loss totals. The archive verifier combines frozen family links, integer counts and deterministic resampling to reproduce all shareable claims. Its receipt declares its scope. The workstation verifier additionally reopens each private source, choice and checkpoint, and reproduces exact program scoring. If a promised statistic cannot be recomputed from a privacy-safe projection, mark that public claim unverifiable and correct the projection before accepting publication.
