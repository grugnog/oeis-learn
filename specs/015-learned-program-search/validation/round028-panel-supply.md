## How the time estimate is computed

The report uses the original eight-attempt search clocks and the selected program's 100-term scorer clock for trial 05 and retained D on the same 128 historical development cases. It records the number and elapsed time of observed, completed and missing portfolios per stratum. A missing or open portfolio is priced at the registered 60 seconds or its longer observed duration. Missing scorer clocks make the corresponding forecast unavailable. Costs are elapsed workstation estimates. GPU occupancy, startup, checkpoint I/O and host cleanup have separate measurement scopes.
