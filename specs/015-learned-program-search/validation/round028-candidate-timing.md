## What the returned ZIP decides

The public report contains search and scorer time by model, profile and stratum. It includes cases with a selection, completed native/reference checks, actual attempts, elapsed decoding and fitting, peak allocated device memory, sampled GPU busy time and observed coverage. Host wall time includes startup, waiting and cleanup. GPU lease and occupancy overlap wall time. Image build time and filesystem headroom are reported separately.

Each stratum uses the slower of its two observed search times and its two scorer times. The scorer estimate also retains the historical per-case average as a floor. The 32 familiar retention cases use the measured parameter rate. The forecast prices both early looks for both learned arms, the 480 full pilot portfolios and the 800 full portfolios for an earned repeat. It includes the original matched training rate, one save/restore per arm per training segment and a measured cold training-role allowance. It reports estimates for one role per registered arm/look and for eight-case batches.

An incomplete search clock or an incomplete check of a selected program yields a measurement gap. A projected pilot over eight hours yields a cost deficit. A pilot that fits with an unaffordable repeat proceeds to pilot-only qualification. If both fit, the next action is prospective qualification. Measurements on these eight exposed families establish a planning estimate. The scientific comparison, independent repeat decision and production-scale acceptance still require their own evidence.
