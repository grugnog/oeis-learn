# Contract: inherited qualification and controlled studies

**Status**: Prospective execution gates. Creating this plan does not admit a learning attempt, expose final data or authorize long training. The [carryover register](../carryover.md) remains the complete task disposition; this contract defines how to execute its remaining goals without many manual commands.

## One interface, explicit authority

Add a study mode to the same public validation suite:

```bash
python3 scripts/foundation_run.py --engine podman --cpus 16 validate \
  --suite pipeline --phase 6 --study-manifest PATH_TO_ADMITTED_STUDY \
  --output tmp-validation/009-study
```

This is a planned interface, not a command available in this PR. `--phase 6` requires `--study-manifest`; `all-ready` never selects research. The study manifest names already decided arms and their exact inputs, checkpoints, policies, capabilities, budgets and prior evidence. Use existing foundation learning/compare/evaluate implementations through internal stage adapters. Do not create a second trainer or fitter.

The command validates qualification/cost authority, runs every registered arm allowed by that authority, gathers mandatory controls/reports and creates one archive. It can combine independently decided studies in a single manifest if each one's gates and aggregate budget are already fixed. It stops at an undecided recipe/retraining/assistance choice or a final-exposure boundary. Validating a study manifest does not itself grant authority; absent decision/admission fields fail before learning or mounting final inputs.

A final study additionally requires the existing immutable final-plan reference and an explicit final operation in the study command, represented by `--final-plan PATH`. Final authority cannot be inferred from `--phase 6` alone. The inner evaluator still enforces the original sealed/authorized/exposed protocol. A study batch never generates and approves its own final plan.

## Gate ledger

| Gate | Required evidence | If missing/failed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Current runtime/admission | Exactness, final-source independent verification, supported subset and resource/negative fixtures for relevant current closures | Block learning; reopen affected semantic task if needed |
| Current operation/device | Requested actual-HIP updates, relevant cache/decoder equivalence, isolation/containment, full checkpoint/resume and mandatory artifact export | Block affected execution; CPU development may continue |
| Resource policy | Qualified aggregate memory/CPU/thread/queue/cache/cancellation settings and selected pipeline identities | No use of unqualified optimized policy in research |
| Scope-specific cost | All scheduled components estimated for the exact proposed scope, projection at most80% of its frozen finite budget | Block that scope; do not borrow a diverse32 receipt for transfer |
| Learning gate (representation and later learning studies; not the attempt establishing this gate) | Independently reviewed diverse32 qualification with inherited success/completeness rules, from a prospectively admitted attempt | Preserve negative T096; no automatic retry or promotion |
| Study decision/provenance | Frozen hypothesis, arms, seeds, initialization, data, controls, selection/stopping and strict/assisted lineage | Block; no invented demonstration or results-driven seed substitution |
| Final exposure | Immutable post-decision plan with fresh sealed cohort, fixed checkpoints/roster and shared/per-arm budgets | Reject final access; exposed cohorts remain diagnostic for later tuning |

Gate applicability is keyed by study kind. A new `kind=learnability` attempt requires current runtime/admission, operations/device, resource, exact-scope cost, strict provenance and an explicit prospective new-attempt decision; it does not require an earlier learnability PASS. Representation and dependent learning studies additionally require that PASS. Final access always requires its separate final-plan authority. Read-only preparation/report fixtures do not need a trained-model learning gate but cannot execute a study by calling themselves preparation.

A stage can complete the gate report while its scientific gate remains blocked. Record both statuses. Current-source evidence reuse follows the dependency contract; old PASS receipts are not universal certificates.

## Preserved scientific rules

The existing [experiment contract](../../008-bigint-constant-synthesis/contracts/experiments.md) remains normative for numerical/provenance/research semantics, except for explicitly versioned scheduling/selection and prospective resource/cadence decisions recorded in the new study contract. Any changed candidate selection is disclosed as part of the study's effective policy and matched across controls; do not compare old v1 and new v2 as if policy were identical.

Keep visible20 versus continuation100 distinct. Checkpoints/curriculum use allowed visible evidence; hidden continuation values cannot rank candidates or select checkpoints. All fixed-denominator failures and missing cases contribute to their declared metrics. Exact-fitting equal-budget portfolios include generation, solving and visible independent verification; one raw attempt is not an equal-cost comparator. Scoring checks the one sealed selected program, not whichever candidate later matches hidden truth.

Transfer gate remains: at least95% complete coverage per development split for every seed; independent verification of every admission; at least8/128 extra structural successes against each shuffled/absent/template comparator in at least two of three seeds and mean paired advantage at least6.25 percentage points; no more than4/128 mean parameter-dev loss against the declared literal/full-input control; frozen descriptive family uncertainty and separate seed spread. Check once at the declared endpoint; no favorable-seed replacement or adaptive final-cohort probing.

A negative/inconclusive study is a valid deliverable and may stop dependent research. A prospective deferral can narrow optional final-study claims, never waive mandatory correctness, learning, cost or hidden-isolation gates. Final cohort freshness is consumed before evaluation and survives interruption. Resume can finish only an identical authorized plan within its remaining ledger; changed-plan retuning requires a new cohort for fresh claims.

## Budgets and operator handoff

Research durations are set by the measured exact-scope manifest, not by copying the24-hour engineering-validation ceiling. There is deliberately no default unlimited or multi-day study budget. Before execution, require finite per-arm and shared wall/work/disk allowances, a scope-correct cost receipt, cleanup/export reserves and explicit stop rules. Missing amounts are invalid, not runtime auto-tuning opportunities.

The phase handoff supplies one exact command for the already admitted batch, its expected duration/range from the cost receipt, hard limits and output archive. User-run HIP steps are combined with all ready applicable checks where possible. No operator must manually sequence raw evaluation, fitting, controls, archive creation and comparison reporting. A subsequent explicit decision is required for12-hour-plus training even if readiness is positive; the validator never launches it just to complete the task list.
