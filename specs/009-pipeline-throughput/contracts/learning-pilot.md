## Learning analysis and disposition

Use nonpadding-token-weighted teacher-forcing loss/accuracy, canonical-target
exact match, full-stream minute bins, fixed-panel train/dev gaps, unique/repeated
examples, effective epochs, actual tokens/updates and role costs. Independently
verified exact100-index continuation is the capability metric. Each arm reports
success/N through(success+unresolved)/N, complete negatives, regressions, paired
changes, families/strata and correct-minus-control changes. One-attempt shuffled/
absent and eight-attempt correct/template budgets are explicitly unequal.

Descriptive existing family resampling uses seed20261016 only for complete pairs;
it is not seed variance or a fresh-final population confidence interval. At least
four comparable nonzero token exposures with coverage at least95%, the same
complete-scored roster/coverage at each point, strictly
increasing exposure, no negative segment slope, a held-out later-checkpoint
backtest error at most5 percentage points, and no worsening dev loss are required
for conditional2x/4x-token scenarios. Publish plateau, recent median per-doubling
trend, diminishing gain, slope/window sensitivity and clipping. Otherwise UNKNOWN.
Actual last-three-interval learner-role seconds/token ranges give conditional
additional learner time and cost per additional trend-predicted verified sequence.
Zero/negative gain has no finite cost ratio. Future evaluation and total study
cost remain UNKNOWN until separately measured; no future run is authorized.
There is no guaranteed nontrivial capacity upper bound from this finite pilot.

The report preserves missing milestones and source-bound failure evidence.
Complete pilot investigation can include zero successes; review must decide
whether more same-regime data/compute is supported, curriculum merits a separately
designed study, or evidence is inconclusive. Original research tasks/defaults are
not automatically closed or promoted by this supplemental result.
