# Pilot evaluation repair and controlled generalization studies

## Implemented scope

The observed pilot fits its training corpus but lacks reliable semantic coverage and a functioning template baseline. Preserve the admitted v3 corpus and historical scores. Repaired results have new source and evaluation-policy bindings.

1. Classify explicit parameters through validated codec tokens. Roundtrip all eight templates and independently admit an affine-template solution in native qualification.
2. Export first canonical error position (zero-based), integer/operator denominators and errors, top-two logit margins and target log probability. Keep hidden values and raw logits out of public diagnostics.
3. Add optional ordinal 6 for the producer's selected checkpoint. Bind the original public selected pointer, checkpoint file hash, weights hash and experiment contract. Load only weights; compute its parameter digest in the probe and require no mutation. Preserve ordinals 0-5.
4. Add two prospective matched eight-attempt policies: `portfolio-clock60-matched/v2` and `portfolio-clock60-matched-drain/v2`. Both isolate the first raw verification from fitting; the latter limits prompt groups to native workers and drains native tasks before GPU decoding. Keep original 60-second portfolio clocks and all unresolved outcomes.
5. Provide fresh 25M/50M-token screens for uniform row sampling, uniform family sampling and cosine LR decay. All three arms share source, corpus, model initialization, token cap and maximum six-hour learner clock. Each arm evaluates its best development-loss weights with the repaired matched policy. Every learner sees only the generic visible corpus. Save best/latest weights plus optimizer/RNG state privately; no resume is authorized.
6. Retain the public-evidence analyzer and research briefs. The temporary HIP helper runs qualification, probes, policy comparisons and fresh screens in series, stops on the first failed command, and refuses an existing output root.

## Research decisions and promotion

Require at least 95% completed measurements per primary split/arm, full-denominator semantic yield, family-macro reporting and confirmation across seeds before promoting a training policy. A source/profile change invalidates historical qualification for execution; each new invocation requalifies the installed source. Do not use final-cohort outcomes for selection. Conditional completed-pair comparisons cannot eliminate cost-dependent censoring.

## Separate larger specifications

- **Data coverage:** version a new producer profile with stage, size and family quotas; disjoint structural holdouts for every stage; generic refresh and behavior-overlap rejection. The original structural cohort has only stage A and neither development cohort has loops. Compare new and historical cohorts separately. Accept only a corpus that fills every declared quota, with preparation cost and rejection reasons exported.
- **Adaptive verified learning:** compare static family balancing with learning-progress sampling and verified search-distillation/replay. Use timestamped batch-level competence, an exploration floor and training-only verification. The historical SYMPLE success-sorted history must not manufacture forgetting signals. RL is a separate ablation.
- **Synthesis interface:** first compare literal and parameterized targets with the repaired fitter; then scope compact AST/recurrence actions or learned abstractions. Attribute gains between neural structure proposals and constant fitting. A new DSL needs execution, codec, canonicalization and checkpoint contracts before a training comparison.

These are alternatives requiring their own reviewed designs, rather than three simultaneous architecture replacements. The four copy-ready academic research prompts and verified starting papers are in `docs/pilot-analysis-20261028.md`.
