# Targets, uncertainty and bounded contingencies

Added 2026-11-08 following operator review. This contract supplements the experiment/execution contracts; it does not weaken exact acceptance or relabel historical results. Its purpose is to distinguish a justified next experiment from a justified scientific claim.

## Target classes

Every frozen target has `id`, `class`, `metric`, `scope`, `baseline`, `threshold`, `evidence`, `on_miss`, `allowed_actions` and `budget_ref`. The target registry covers all numeric policy values, not only the examples below. Research defaults are design choices until frozen, not empirically established optimal values.

| Class | Examples | Meaning of a miss |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Integrity invariant — always hard | Exact source values and offsets; independent candidate verification; truth isolation; no budget reset; honest uncertainty/proof labels; current compatible qualification | Reject/quarantine affected evidence and stop the affected operation. No exploratory branch may bypass this |
| Execution limit — hard in the selected scope | User's <=24h training-task ceiling; frozen campaign/resource/search caps; owned outputs; supported numerical/grammar semantics | Stop/checkpoint or reject. A new prospective profile may change ordinary limits, but cannot enlarge this task or exceed the user ceiling |
| Claim/adoption criterion — provisional before freeze, binding afterwards | +2 pp gain, >=95% completion, <=2 pp retention loss; declared uncertainty bound/support; at least two confirmation seeds | The specified claim or automatic adoption fails/is inconclusive. May retain baseline or execute an independently justified, pre-reserved diagnostic; never mark the failed gate PASS |
| Research/utility aspiration — soft | Useful new structures, reduced search cost, broader transfer, informative learning curves, new conjectures | Record degree of progress and opportunity cost. Select a bounded follow-up or defer; a missed aspiration is not a software failure |

A value may occur in more than one class with distinct scopes: 100% of admitted targets must pass independent verification (integrity), while 95% measurement completion is a proposed promotion threshold. The remaining 5% are unresolved tasks, not permission for invalid targets. Once 95% is frozen, a 94.9% run cannot pass that claim, but it can still motivate a reliability diagnostic.

### Classification of current choices

- **Always hard**: correctness, containment, provenance, isolated truth, accounting and truthful labels. Actual HIP is hard evidence for a claimed HIP run, not a claim that every future experiment needs a new device test irrespective of scope.
- **User bound**: each training task <=24 active hours. No internal fallback exceeds it. The 672h full-path reservation is a maximum optional design envelope, not a target to consume or an authorization to launch every task. A smaller selected campaign gets its own lower binding cap.
- **Current comparison profiles**: 20 visible/100 total terms, eight attempts, 60s portfolio, four-second fit and 1,024 generation tokens remain fixed within their comparisons. A witnessed representational limitation can justify a separately frozen language/budget experiment, with cost and fairness disclosed.
- **Proposed scientific thresholds**: +2 pp gain, >=95% completion and <=2 pp retention loss are initial meaningfulness/reliability tolerances; a statistically detectable +0.5 pp and an uncertain +5 pp are different outcomes. Neither automatically satisfies the +2 pp confirmation rule. Two seeds are a minimum replication requirement for that rule, not assurance that training variability is understood.
- **Proposed study-size/design values**: 128 tasks and >=16 groups per primary stratum; 16 screening trials; up to three nonbaseline finalists; 4M/25M/100M/400M tokens; 20/10/70 curriculum fractions; 25% OEIS replay; yield thresholds; 32 candidate source statistics. Calibrate or revise prospectively based on feasibility and the scientific question. They are not goals that justify extra work merely to reach a count.
- **Operational defaults**: 30-minute finalization reserve, shard/draw/archive caps and specific model sizes can be recalibrated before freeze; they become hard ceilings/floors for that scope. Do not waive cleanup reserve mid-run. The <=60s progress requirement is operational acceptance, with explicit stalled/unavailable events allowed as specified.
- **Delivery obligations**: implement and validate the complete five-stage capability, including negative cases. Successful OEIS adaptation, a useful learned library, or a genuinely novel theorem are research aspirations, not guaranteed software deliverables.

## Added soft targets and optional efficiency claim

Record these goals against measured baselines; none silently replaces the primary endpoint:

| Goal | Proposed soft target / observation | Consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Compute utility | >=20% lower total measured cost for comparable capability, or an explicitly quantified capability gain worth its added cost | Consider the prospectively registered efficiency route; otherwise descriptive result |
| Generation tails | >=20% fewer token-cap events per fixed set of attempts without loss of verified yield; also report tail wall time | Prioritize termination/representation work if unsuccessful; never hide failures by cutting off earlier |
| Breadth | Positive per-family effects beyond the existing three-family concentration; aim for gains in at least two additional independent groups, showing regressions too | Supports broader curriculum investment; not a substitute for overall paired evidence |
| Search-to-learning transfer | More independently verified unseen-task successes per fixed search budget after an update cycle | If archive grows but this does not, change replay/diversity or stop self-learning expansion |
| Useful abstraction | Components reused on multiple held-out groups, with >=20% reduction in charged search/execution cost as an initial aspiration | If only surface source length improves, retain analysis-only components |
| Measurement efficiency | Target evaluation/monitoring <=25% of active screening/learning task time, with mandatory checks preserved | Reduce optional cadence or use prospectively frozen smaller screening panels; report uncertainty/cost trade-off |
| Decision quality | Outcome-blind sensitivity simulations can distinguish practically relevant gains under plausible dependence/censoring; aim for >=80% detection in the declared design scenarios | If not, change panel/looks before freeze or label the pilot exploratory; do not promise nominal power from 16 groups alone |

An **optional efficiency adoption route** must be explicitly selected before outcomes. It requires the same integrity, completion, replication and retention rules, >=20% measured total-cost reduction on the frozen workload, and an adjusted conservative capability lower bound >=-1 pp against baseline for each designated primary stratum. Cost includes preparation/discovery/extraction and all charged execution within the frozen scope; if amortized, freeze reuse horizon and also report unamortized cost. Repeated timing workloads and uncertainty/repeatability rules must be fixed before measurement. This route supports only an efficiency/noninferiority claim; it does not establish an accuracy gain or automatically justify harder-task scaling. If it was not registered, an attractive cost result can motivate a new confirmation study but cannot retroactively adopt this endpoint.

## Separate evidence verdict from action

Store two independent fields:

- `evidence_verdict`: CONFIRMED_GAIN, CONFIRMED_EFFICIENCY, NO_MATERIAL_GAIN, REGRESSION, INCONCLUSIVE, INCOMPLETE or INVALID.
- `next_action`: ADVANCE_REGISTERED, RETAIN_BASELINE, EXPLORE_RESERVED, DEFER_BRANCH or STOP_AFFECTED.

Existing gate codes map explicitly: an unmet +2 pp/uncertainty criterion maps to NO_MATERIAL_GAIN or INCONCLUSIVE based on its frozen precision rule, not an assertion of zero learning. A completed negative experiment is mechanically complete. A verified software feature can remain useful when its research branch is deferred. INVALID evidence cannot justify any candidate-dependent experiment until the fault is repaired; an unaffected baseline branch can proceed with its own valid evidence.

EXPLORE_RESERVED requires all of: intact integrity, a named unresolved decision, recorded evidence/alternative explanations, a prospectively allowed trigger and diagnostic design, a preallocated task/resource/panel slot, no assessment use, explicit expected distinguishing observations, and a terminal decision rule. It never changes the previous gate or upgrades a candidate. Training loss improvement alone is insufficient: require a declared additional signal such as improving held-out executable yield below the materiality threshold, identified seed disagreement, or a witnessed representation/runtime bottleneck.

Further diagnostic choices based on results are exposed development work. If a diagnostic chooses a configuration, confirmation must use the already reserved independent panel or a new prospective cohort. Do not repeatedly inspect confirmation until a desired threshold is met. Software acceptance covers every branch using fixtures; live research takes only eligible branches.

The frozen comparison count includes optional confirmation looks and alternative claim routes on the same panel, including a triggered third-seed decision. A diagnostic reservation does not confer extra unadjusted hypothesis tests. A new confirmation cohort must be independently reserved before its outcomes; it does not erase the selection history.

## Bounded contingency reserve

Before freezing, reallocate **24h of the existing 192h synthetic-scaling reservation** into an explicit diagnostic pool: 168h routine scaling plus 24h contingency. Total training upper bound remains 536h and full campaign upper bound remains 672h. This is not additive. Routine scaling still has at most eight predeclared task slots, each <=24h, with their cumulative ceilings now <=168h. If calibration predicts insufficient routine allowance for 400M, stop at the attainable milestone or prospectively redesign the campaign; do not assume the contingency pool is spare scaling time.

The full-path default offers at most four diagnostic tasks, each <=12h and aggregate <=24h, shared across all contingency rows below, with at most one diagnostic episode per failure class in a campaign. A paired extra seed can consume two tasks. Qualification/evaluation/packaging must fit the corresponding existing campaign envelopes; allocate exact ancillary reservations too. A capability/diversity-only campaign does not inherit this full-path pool: explicitly freeze a smaller pool by reallocating its own unspent planned work, or stop with a proposal. No frozen task can be enlarged after outcomes. No recursive diagnostic-to-diagnostic loop.

Branches with changes to language, model shape, dataset membership or optimization policy require the existing compatible child-study/qualification rules. If not already concretely frozen, publish a proposal and halt that branch; an unused reservation alone does not define an experiment or authorize a guessed implementation.

## Decision and contingency register

| Uncertainty / observed outcome | Distinguish first | Bounded response | Exit / fallback |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Very weak baseline learning | Can a tiny independently verified, expressible training panel be memorized and conditioned on? Are execution/fitting failures dominating? | One tiny real-update/conditioning diagnostic, followed by fixed-candidate acceptance checks where indicated | Repair implementation/representation if broken; do not start broad HPO on an unusable endpoint |
| Fresh structures do not improve transfer | Was there actual structural novelty? Is difficulty excessive, exposure insufficient, or old-skill retention lost? | One matched static/refresh difficulty-mixture pilot with identical allocation policy and recorded grouping; choose only a predeclared alternate mix | Retain static/hybrid support if negative; tuning may continue on the valid baseline. Refresh is not a prerequisite for every later stage |
| D-derived allocation underperforms | Is the reward too noisy/delayed, or are bins mismatched to task difficulty? | One fixed-stream/allocation control on the same prepared support; do not simultaneously change data and language | Keep the simpler allocation if justified; no mandatory full B/D replication |
| Short HPO rankings fail to predict longer runs | Compare the reserved unpruned controls at short and long milestones; identify rank reversal and wall-limited partials | Freeze a new smaller search with longer fidelity/grace, or extend one predeclared candidate; preserve failed/pruned history | Prefer baseline or confirmed finalist. Do not keep pruning with a discredited proxy or consume trials to reach 16 |
| Model is slower but more capable, or cheaper without accuracy gain | Compare equal-token and equal-wall-time curves, startup/inference cost and memory headroom | Use registered efficiency route or retain a capability/cost frontier for a new study | No single scalar winner chosen after inspecting outcomes; a larger model is not automatically progress |
| Gain is promising but <2 pp or interval crosses zero | Small effect versus insufficient groups, censoring, multiplicity or between-seed disagreement | One preregistered extension of training OR one independent confirmation/seed expansion appropriate to the uncertainty, not an arbitrary repeat | Keep original INCONCLUSIVE/no-material-gain status; adopt only under subsequent valid evidence |
| One seed improves and another regresses | Separate seed variation from task/censoring variation | A preallocated paired third seed, same settings/data/budgets; use its registered aggregate rule and report all seeds | Original two-seed gate remains failed. Default predeclared three-seed diagnostic rule: mean gain >=2 pp over all three seeds, at least two positive seed effects, no seed worse than -2 pp, and the same per-arm/seed completion, retention and adjusted conditional-family uncertainty checks. Report all three; never choose the best two. This is a separate registered decision, not a rescue of the original two-seed gate |
| 95% completion or 2 pp retention criterion misses narrowly | Are cases censored by scheduling, solver limits or model token tails? Conservative retention bounds may fail merely from unresolved baseline cases | Fixed-input reliability replay under original scientific caps; no learning on confirmation-case solutions. Any caps change gets a separate comparison | Keep old result incomplete; use known qualified drain and valid baseline. Never relabel an unknown as a completed failure |
| Sparse fresh groups or unreliable intervals | Availability, dependency structure, group sizes, baseline variability and attainable detection after planned multiple looks | Outcome-blind census/sensitivity study before scores; prospectively change panel, reduce looks or restrict the claim to a domain | Diagnostic/exploratory result or deferred branch, not silently lowered quotas or pooled strata |
| Long training plateaus | Training-only fit versus held-out execution, overconfidence, termination tails and gains per incremental compute | One predeclared medium-length continuation only if additional evidence supports delayed learning; otherwise one data/representation diagnostic | Stop at the last useful checkpoint. 100M/400M are possible milestones, not obligations |
| OEIS yield is too low or highly repetitive | Candidate coverage, independent verification rejection, language coverage, target diversity and discovery cost | Narrow an outcome-blind training pool or improve mechanical preparation/search in a new bounded pilot; disclose exposure. No imported formulas | Keep synthetic learning/search-only discovery. OEIS self-learning can be deferred without blocking component software acceptance |
| Replay improves solved training tasks but not new tasks | Duplicate dominance, selection bias, synthetic forgetting or poor target weighting | One preset lower replay fraction/diversity-balanced pilot against continued synthetic control | Retain synthetic model or search archive without distillation; no reward for archive size alone |
| Components compress syntax but do not help | Fully expanded cost, cross-group reuse, search overhead and effective fit support | Compare search-only reuse with expanded-program distillation under matched budgets | Keep validated components as tools or defer adoption; do not force a new token vocabulary |
| External database offers too few useful tasks | Exact integer semantics, full enumeration cost, related-object grouping and appropriate task horizon | Publish feasibility/suitability result; at most one separately frozen compatible source/profile pilot | Defer ingestion. No padding, rounded values or arbitrary ordering to meet quotas |
| Finite match lacks proof or novelty | Counterexamples, known constructions, supported proof class and assumptions | Bounded independent verification/literature review; novel-method claim remains separate | Publish finite/conjectural evidence honestly; absence of proof/novelty is not failed learning |
| Correctness, leakage or recovery accounting fails | Identify scope and affected lineage before interpreting scores | Quarantine affected artifacts; fix and requalify; retain consumed budgets and all prior evidence | Hard stop for affected execution. No soft target or reserve may override this |

## Diagnostic sensitivity and review

Before expensive confirmation, exercise the frozen gate with outcome-blind synthetic scenarios: true gains 0, 2 and 5 pp; varied family correlation/size, seed disagreement, 0/2/5/10% censoring and the planned comparison count. Report false-advance frequency, missed-gain frequency and inconclusive fraction conditional on those assumptions. A 2 pp target may be undetectable with only 16 groups, particularly when conservative censoring costs exceed the gain. This does not justify pretending those intervals are precise. Revise support/looks prospectively or use the result only for exploratory allocation. Simulations do not establish real-data independence or population seed confidence.

Distinguish detection of a positive effect from passing the complete promotion rule. At a true effect exactly equal to the 2 pp point-estimate cutoff, that cutoff alone can reject roughly half of symmetric estimates even with high precision. The 80% aspiration therefore applies only to explicitly named detectable scenarios (for example, a 5 pp effect); it is not an 80% promotion guarantee at the materiality boundary.

For every completed stage, publish target values/classes, observed values, uncertainty and missingness, evidence verdict, selected action, rejected alternatives, remaining budget and the information the next action is expected to resolve. Keep the full candidate cost/capability frontier, including negative results. No new research is launched by this planning amendment.
