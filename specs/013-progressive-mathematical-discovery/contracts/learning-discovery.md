# Learning targets, components and discoveries

Proposed contract; not implemented. Authority: FR-012–016, FR-027–037.

## Mechanical targets

A new typed AST generator has a sealed numeric/operator/control-flow law and counter-keyed draws. Render to bigint-body, independently parse/type-check, verify exact 100-term values and cross-check native execution. Admission excludes unsupported semantics, overflow/profile violations, repeated/held-out source groups and excessive work. Rejected draws remain in the ledger. Safe `paired_learning_view` abstraction may replace eligible constants only after control-taint and parameter-count checks; ground it back to the original concrete source and verify equivalence. Otherwise retain whole-row literal fallback.

Generate sealed finite shards, not an unrestricted background corpus. Cap draws per shard, accepted bytes, structure counts and execution; initial default is 4,096 accepted rows or 65,536 draws per shard, with a 256 MiB uncompressed shard cap. The whole campaign freezes the number of available shards and a separate total draw/work cap. Required diversity shortfall stops admission. Preserve fixed train-only retention/probe banks and report active operator/loop/branch behavior using independent execution, not syntax counts alone.

## OEIS search and admission

The custodian freezes an exact bulk snapshot and source terms/offsets. Training generator sees only the first 20 terms. The admission role independently checks final concrete candidates at all 100 admitted indices; the 80 non-visible terms are training feedback, not final assessment. Require a complete exact source horizon for this profile; short source entries are ineligible. Known formulas/metadata are not targets or scaffolds.

Initial yield audit: at most 512 training tasks selected by frozen outcome-blind rules, eight candidates/task, the ordinary 60s portfolio cap, at most 12 active CPU/GPU task hours including admission and packaging. This work belongs to the first OEIS task reservation. Gate defaults: at least 32 accepted distinct task groups and 16 distinct canonical program structures; at least 0.05 accepted task groups per measured active search/admission minute; no invalid acceptance. Freeze counts and costs before outcomes. If membership cannot supply this pool, report admission deficit before model search. These are conservative pilot design choices, not claims about current D yield.

Native and independent reference execution must agree. Prefix match, solver SAT, or native success alone is insufficient. Hash admission evidence to source snapshot/member, concrete source, exact indices, numerical limits and reference/native implementation identities. A proof of all-domain equivalence is not required for a finite training target; retain the finite scope explicitly.

Create a separate archive type rather than bypassing the existing mechanical replay validator. Initial capacity 4,096 targets, at most two distinct verified programs/task; sample task first then alternative. Retain shortest verified representation as one target and a structurally distinct alternative when available, with deterministic ties. Bound concrete length by the profile and record runtime. No “shorter than unknown OEIS oracle” requirement. Convert accepted concrete programs into safe parameterized targets only by verified grounding; literal fallback remains valid.

Initial replay mixture: 25% admitted OEIS examples, 75% synthetic support, measured by charged target tokens with whole-example overshoot recorded. Freeze scheduler, capacity, freshness and deterministic eviction before discovery. Same starting weights/optimizer-compatible child initialization for replay and continued-synthetic arms, paired seeds, matched charged-token milestones and inference allowance. The control does not receive discovered targets. Report additional discovery cost separately and in total utility; model training comparisons are not equal-total-compute claims.

Discovery acceptance has a real search/admission/update path. A tiny known acceptance fixture may force a verified candidate to test mechanics but must be DIAGNOSTIC_ONLY with zero claimed autonomous discoveries. The live yield audit requires actual checkpoint-generated candidates and records rejected ones.

## Reusable components

Initial component profile: typed pure bigint expressions over permitted arithmetic/index/explicit arguments, no hidden mutable locals or statements. Mine repeated connected subtrees across at least three distinct verified training structures and two task groups. Use deterministic alpha-normalized typed holes; component semantics are defined by hygienic expansion in operand order. No reordering, algebraic simplification, recursion or unbound captures is implicitly permitted.

Freeze caps: 64 components/library, dependency depth <=4, <=8 typed arguments/component, <=4,096 nodes and 256 KiB in any expanded candidate; the lower existing runtime/resource bound wins. Reject expansion bombs and cyclic dependencies before fitting. Immutable component definitions may refer only to prior/acyclic components; inspect the fully expanded candidate through the independent parser/reference and native acceptance.

A bounded library-guided proposal policy composes these fragments with ordinary typed AST search, using model scores where available. Expanded candidates go through the same source codec/fitter/evaluator. Accepted expanded programs can be distilled through supervised training. This provides actual learned reusable search and subsequent learning without new library-token embeddings. Archive/library versions bind checkpoints even though the decoder vocabulary is unchanged.

Default component comparison: paired seeds, same initial checkpoint and synthetic/data allowances, library and no-library search portfolios of eight attempts/60s. Within library portfolios reserve at most four attempt slots for component-guided proposals; no-library receives all eight ordinary slots. Charge extraction/verification, expanded tokens/nodes and execution, not just short surface names. Report discovery cost plus downstream training/search cost. No component from evaluation witnesses, confirmation failures or assessment outputs may enter the library.

Compact component tokens, mutable statement macros and new call semantics are later separately qualified profiles, not required to implement this initial stage. Real acceptance must demonstrate a mined component affecting a candidate and a verified update, not only offline compression statistics.

## Claims and refutation

Seal a candidate before novelty analysis. A dossier includes exact source/library, source snapshot and offset, generation/selection budget, tested/untested range, independent execution, known-baseline comparison and proof/novelty records. External literature lookup is analysis-only and cannot silently feed training or candidate selection.

Implement a narrow new bigint polynomial certificate adapter first: exact coefficient normalization over mathematical integers, independent from production lowering, with assumptions and a bounded certificate budget. Source binding is mandatory. A polynomial identity certificate does not prove unbounded checked-machine execution safety; either supply a separate verified range argument over a declared finite domain or keep that limitation explicit. Unsupported loop/recurrence claims remain conjectural or finitely verified. Existing legacy certificate labels do not qualify the new language.

Novelty states are not_reviewed, known, uncertain, potentially_novel, independently_confirmed. A model-generated expression does not trigger the last state. Independent confirmation includes named reviewer/evidence and a scoped comparison against prior work; the system may emit an incomplete dossier awaiting review without claiming discovery.

A counterexample generates an immutable refutation record, quarantines the target and walks reverse dependencies through components, archive versions and consumed-update ledger to all affected checkpoints/dossiers. Do not delete adverse evidence. Re-training from a clean ancestor is a new charged recovery path, never erasure of contaminated exposure.

## External mathematical sources

First pilot: combinatorial-short/v1, separately reported from the primary benchmark. Freeze up to 32 allowlisted permutation statistics and first/second moments over complete enumerated collections for n=0..8. Visible n=0..3, admission or evaluation n=4..8. Preserve factorial collection cardinalities, offset, exact totals and zero-size conventions; independently compute checks with an approved reviewed adapter. Do not execute arbitrary downloaded statistic code. Bound total enumerated objects/work/bytes before admission.

Source metadata and definitions remain custodian-only; human choice of transformation is disclosed as external_assisted. Related statistics, maps and distribution-equivalent transformations stay grouped across roles. Learner targets are its own independently verified candidate programs, not imported statistic implementations. The new profile requires dedicated length-bucketed four-term learner and generator batches, input encoding/collation, loss, target/prompt projections and native/reference scoring adapters. Existing non-20-row rejection remains for the historical profile. Masked tensor padding, if any, is not an authoritative numerical term and must not affect losses or visibility. Qualify a real four-input forward/backward, save/restore, generation and score path; no numeric padding to 20/100. Its accuracy cannot satisfy the main scaling gate. For a mixture learning pilot require at least four independent training source groups and four disjoint evaluation groups with at least two eligible tasks each; otherwise deliver feasibility/deficit evidence and stop that research branch. Software acceptance covers both outcomes.

LMFDB/Cremona and KnotInfo/Dartmouth get source suitability records: immutable acquisition/access terms, integer versus algebraic/approximate values, index/relationship semantics, finite support, computation cost and independent verification options. Zeta-zero approximations are ineligible for exact integer profiles. Finite polynomial coefficients are not treated as an infinite sequence completed by zeros. Additional ingestion needs a new concrete task profile and bounded matched utility pilot.
