# Research review for controlled generalization experiments

Reviewed 2026-10-30 against main `fba656c4147484e4a8383eab99a0a0599bf20771` (PR #61). This is the evidence and rationale for [spec.md](spec.md), not a new experiment result.

The four supplied reports were treated as research leads, not authorities. Their substantive proposals were checked against primary papers and the current implementation. [source-register.md](source-register.md) records reading depth, unavailable sources, and exclusions; [citation-inventory.json](citation-inventory.json) preserves the reports' numbered references. A retrieved paper is not automatically evidence for a requirement. Peripheral sources screened for relevance are distinguished from methods/results read to support a decision. Downloaded papers are not redistributed here.

## Decision

Implement a bounded experimental capability to determine whether better data coverage, refreshed examples, explicit parameterized targets, or training-only verified replay improve **useful conditional exact synthesis** at declared cost. Preserve the repaired evaluation, historical cohort and exact fitter. Separate the effects instead of shipping a combined purported cure.

Priorities are: extract available evidence; establish a separately frozen challenge benchmark and selection protocol; run isolated data and target comparisons; enable a small, independently selectable replay comparison. Do not require reinforcement learning, a new architecture, an expanded language, or a new numerical optimizer.

## Primary research: supported transfers and boundaries

### Data and target representation

**Biggio et al., [Neural Symbolic Regression that Scales](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/biggio21a/biggio21a.pdf), §§3–5 and evaluation definitions.** The method trains on synthetic skeletons, varying constants and sampled observations, then fits continuous constants. The evaluation distinguishes seen skeletons with new numeric instantiations from disjoint skeletons. This supports separating refresh within a family from expansion of structural support. Its continuous expressions, BFGS and benchmark definitions do not establish exact-integer, loop or branch behavior. A finite precomputed skeleton collection with resampled constants is not unlimited structural novelty.

**Kamienny et al., [End-to-end Symbolic Regression with Transformers](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2022/file/42eb37cdbefd7abae0835f4b67548c39-Paper-Conference.pdf), method and Table 2 ablations.** Predicting constants as well as structure can improve refinement initialization over skeleton-only prediction in that setting. This directly undermines a universal claim that neural models cannot usefully predict constants. It motivates an experiment between the existing literal and parameterized representations, with comparable fitting for both. Continuous tolerance-based recovery is not exact integer continuation.

**d'Ascoli et al., [Deep Symbolic Regression for Recurrent Sequences](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v162/d-ascoli22a/d-ascoli22a.pdf), §§2–4.** Synthetic integer/recurrent expression generation and numeric tokenization provide a relevant precedent for learning constants and recurrence structure. The expression language, very large training regime, and finite extrapolation protocol differ from this project's bounded imperative language and 100-term exact assessment. Neither its scale nor its accuracy can be imported as a workstation forecast.

**Otte et al., [Improving Neural Symbolic Regression with a Diverse Prior and Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.17994), §§3–4, especially §4.4.** This October 2026 preprint separates prior and post-training ablations, and also finds a disadvantage for skeleton-only prediction in its setting. It strengthens the case for controlled prior/target comparisons, not a claim that our encoder or fitter is defective. Its continuous objectives, large sampling budgets and refinement costs cannot set this feature's budgets. The paper itself notes uncertainty from its small black-box benchmark and heterogeneous cost comparisons.

### Generalization and repeated exposure

**Keysers et al., [Measuring Compositional Generalization](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1912.09713), §§3–5.** Distribution-based compositionality assessment seeks high compound divergence with low atom divergence. It does not require every family in every size/magnitude cell or all subtrees to be unseen. We adopt explicit operator, composition, length and execution coverage reports. Whole-family novelty alone is not a certificate of compositional isolation; unsupported strata and marginal distribution differences must remain visible.

**Kim and Linzen, [COGS](https://aclanthology.org/2020.emnlp-main.731.pdf), benchmark construction and generalization cases.** Distinct lexical, structural and depth shifts illustrate why a single random holdout is insufficient. Transfer here is the separation of challenge types, not a numerical performance expectation from semantic parsing.

**Muennighoff et al., [Scaling Data-Constrained Language Models](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2305.16264), scaling experiments; Lovelace et al., [Prescriptive Scaling Laws for Data Constrained Training](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.01640), §§3–5.** Repetition can initially be useful, with later diminishing returns or degradation; the newer preprint fits an explicit repetition penalty and disputes allocation implications of the earlier fit. Both study language-model losses under their own data/model regimes. Neither supplies a universal epoch cutoff for this synthesizer. Record actual unique examples, families, exposures, tokens, updates and costs, and test refresh directly rather than extrapolating a natural-language scaling law.

**Power et al., [Grokking](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2201.02177), experiments and regularization ablations; Liu et al., [Omnigrok](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2210.01117), mechanism and teacher–student setup.** These demonstrate delayed generalization in particular conditions, not a theorem that this model must or cannot grok. The current learner uses AdamW with its default weight decay; “no regularization” is factually inappropriate. The pilot supports deprioritizing a longer unchanged run, not declaring a permanent memorization state.

### Search, verification and distillation

**Gauthier and Urban, [Learning Program Synthesis for Integer Sequences from Scratch](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2202.11908), search/learning methods and experiments.** This is a relevant random-start search–check–learn precedent. However, it searches across OEIS itself, retains discovered solutions for learning, and does not establish a clean family-held-out strict-track result for our setting. Its 993 initial solutions followed by 8,438 additional solutions should not be rewritten as 8,438 total; the reported CPU expenditure is not simply bootstrap cost. Its small-network execution budget is not a suitable limit for our neural generation plus exact fitting.

**Gauthier, Olšák and Urban, [Alien Coding](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2301.11479), §§2, 5–6, and the [AITP 2023 abstract](https://aitp-conference.org/2023/abstract/AITP_2023_paper_8.pdf).** Later work adds NMT, wide beam search, smallest/fastest solutions and language extensions over long evolving runs. This is not one isolated replay ablation. Of 40,577 solutions with additional terms, 14,701 small programs time out; the 90.57% generalization figure is among non-timeouts. This is a concrete reason to retain our full denominators. Borrow verified learning and bounded retention, not imported OEIS programs, altered language primitives, or survivor-only success rates.

**Ellis et al., [DreamCoder](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/~ellisk/documents/dreamcoder_with_supplement.pdf), wake/sleep methods and component ablations.** Recognition training, replay, sampled tasks and library abstraction jointly improve held-out task solving. The abstraction mechanism changes the language and has its own contribution. This supports treating representation/library changes separately from replay. It does not establish that a replay-only intervention repairs conditioning.

**Butt et al., [CodeIt: Self-Improving Language Models with Prioritized Hindsight Replay](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mlresearch/v235/main/assets/butt24a/butt24a.pdf), §§3–4 and ablations.** CodeIt uses pretrained CodeT5+, human ARC programs and mutations, and iterative search on demonstrations of the evaluation tasks. Hindsight pairs a valid program with the input/output task it actually produces, rather than calling a failed target solved. Its replay results justify a bounded verified alternative-target hypothesis, while its provenance and adaptation protocol do not transfer to our strict held-out evaluation. Cumulative solutions and performance of the current policy must remain distinct.

**Zelikman et al., [STaR](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2203.14465), §3; Singh et al., [ReST-EM](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2312.06585), algorithm and §5.** These use verified model-generated outputs for supervised training. STaR's answer-conditioned rationalization and ReST-EM's large pretrained models differ materially from our setting. Their standard procedures restart training from a base model each iteration; this is not evidence that arbitrary continuous replay has equivalent behavior. ReST-EM caps successful examples per problem to mitigate easy-task domination. Adopt that balancing concern, compare against continued supervised training, and count search cost.

**Shao et al., [DeepSeekMath](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2402.03300), §4.1; Schulman et al., [PPO](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1707.06347), §3.** GRPO normalizes rewards within a group. Identical binary rewards provide no relative correctness signal (a zero-variance case requires handling). A supervised plateau therefore does not make GRPO necessary or sufficient. PPO's clipped objective is a surrogate; clipping does not make arbitrary stale-buffer updates unbiased or universally monotonic. Recent off-policy papers address differing assumptions and bias/variance tradeoffs, not that universal guarantee. Reinforcement learning is deferred.

### Structural diagnostics versus guarantees

**Park et al., [Grammar-Aligned Decoding](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2405.21047), probability formulation; [Constrained Sampling for Language Models Should Be Easy](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2506.05754), constrained-distribution formulation.** Grammar masking can distort a desired sampling distribution. This does not diagnose why the current fitter timed out or prove that grammar alignment improves exact continuation. Keep validity constraints and measure source diversity before changing decoding.

**Smith and Albarghouthi, [Program Synthesis with Equivalence Reduction](https://pages.cs.wisc.edu/~aws/papers/vmcai19.pdf), motivation and equivalence assumptions.** Proven rewrite equivalence and observational agreement on finite examples are different. Use finite fingerprints for declared duplicate screening, not as an unbounded equivalence claim or a proof that two structurally different programs cannot generalize similarly. Learned tree-distance/verifier papers remain diagnostic inspiration, not authority to accept or reject exact programs without verification.

## Corrections required before using the reports

| Report claim or prescription | Independent check | Specification decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A continuous BFGS/LM optimizer dominates this pilot; Jacobian/singularity telemetry is required. | The actual fitter uses bounded exact affine elimination followed by bounded SMT. No such floating optimizer is used in this path. | Instrument actual phase/status/reason and queue/service/cleanup events. No invented residuals or Jacobians. |
| Abstract all constants; differentiability or a new integer solver guarantees successful grounding. | Control-sensitive constants require whole-program dependence analysis; repeated multipliers can be nonlinear in a parameter. Resource limits and unsupported cases remain. | Reuse conservative parameterization and exact bounded fitting. Test representation choice; do not relax control protection. |
| The absent-input control is an unconditional language model; shuffled inputs prove a complete causal account. | Absent input is synthetic encoder memory. Shuffling deranges encoder memories across prompts; it does not permute terms within each sequence. Fitting still sees the correct visible prefix. | Describe interventions literally. Add a reusable prompt-independent proposal bank with explicit costs; make no encoder-collapse claim. |
| The fitter matches withheld data. | Candidate generation, fitting and selection use visible terms; hidden terms belong to scoring. | Reject hidden-data access, including proposed semantic checkpoint selection using evaluation truth. |
| A broader benchmark needs every family at every magnitude and all subtrees unseen. | The generator may not support such combinations; DBCA imposes a different criterion. | Declare feasible marginal quotas and measured composition novelty; fail a shortfall instead of silently redistributing it. |
| The final model can never generalize and balancing has been disproved generally. | Pilot results cover a particular cohort, schedule and seed; the papers do not provide that guarantee. | Register specific hypotheses and inconclusive outcomes; preserve successful and unsuccessful comparisons. |
| Replay or GRPO will necessarily restore conditioning. | None of the reviewed studies tests this intervention in our exact strict-track setup. | Isolated supervised replay comparison and matched conditioning controls; no RL prerequisite. |
| Citation labels establish the claimed experiment. | Report 1 cites the Differentiable Fixer while discussing Execution-Guided synthesis; it calls L2G-SR's work LLM-SRBench. A recommender-agent paper is unrelated to its conditioning explanation. | Use actual primary titles/settings, and exclude inaccessible or irrelevant evidence. |

## Experimental and delivery choices

Training/selection supervision and sealed assessment must have distinct identities from the outset. A dedicated mechanically generated selection cohort can supply selection feedback; it is explicitly **not** a hidden evaluation cohort. Its programs/targets cannot enter replay. Assessment continuations remain unavailable to every selection decision. Already exposed historical results remain exploratory. This respects constitution V without an amendment.

Use three paired seeds for a confirmatory comparison, but do not run a full Cartesian product or all full-length research experiments merely to qualify software. The default 25M-token cap and 4M/8M/16M/25M checkpoint milestones are engineering choices motivated by pilot scale, not literature-derived optima. Tokens are the primary training budget; examples and optimizer steps are measured, not falsely declared equal. Representation length differences must be reported.

A replay arm requires a common frozen initializer, paired continued-training control, training-only verified discoveries, bounded retention, and complete added generation/fitting cost. It must retain original mechanically generated targets and avoid rewarding only frequent easy families. A gain after extra search is a quality result at that cost, not automatically a compute-efficiency gain.

All difficult algorithm work belongs to one contiguous phase, including invariants, reference cases and algorithm tests. Planning establishes interfaces before that phase and routes later algorithm corrections back into it. Routine integration and packaging follow. Hardware acceptance should take one serial operator invocation with a single compatible qualification receipt, real forward/backward evidence, representative new-path smoke cases and a validated shareable bundle. Actual failure or a changed qualification closure can justify a targeted rerun; the feature must make the reason explicit rather than weakening the gates.
