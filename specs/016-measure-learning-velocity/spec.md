# Feature Specification — Learning velocity on new sequence families

**Feature Branch** `016-measure-learning-velocity` (proposed)  
**Created** 2026-11-26  
**Status** Draft for development handoff  
**Input** Round029 research handoff, repository README and the adopted OEIS Learn constitution. [Handoff][handoff] · [README][readme] · [Constitution][constitution]

**Research decision.** Which change buys the fastest increase in exact solves on new families per million useful training tokens and per charged workstation hour? The learner sees 20 indexed terms. A fitter may recover unknown integer constants from those terms. The selector submits one executable program, and an independent scorer checks all 80 hidden terms. Transfer to previously unseen **real OEIS families** is the goal. Witnessed synthetic families help explain where gains and misses arise.

Round028 ordinary is the reusable **training control**. D, the older adaptive-training checkpoint, is the **operational reference**. At the one-seed 4M endpoint, ordinary solved 22/128 development and 4/32 retention cases. D solved 21/128 and 6/32. The structural difference of +2.83 percentage points had missing-outcome bounds of −14.50 to +11.50. Those pooled counts leave real OEIS transfer unsettled. In a separate audit of 64 structural cases, 51 had a verified witness and a completed search whose admitted programs all missed. The audit found no verified solution passed over by the selector. ## User Scenarios & Testing

### User Story 1 — Decide what can be reused and afforded (Priority P1)

Before booking the learner, the operator needs to know which comparisons can start, what they would cost, and which piece of private evidence would change that decision.

**Independent test.** Present preflight with a matching score, a changed probe and a checkpoint of uncertain origin. It must respectively credit the donor, price re-evaluation and identify the affected control.

**Acceptance scenario.** Given an intact 4M checkpoint and a missing 1M timing record, when the operator requests a curve, the 4M checkpoint stays eligible and the cost report identifies the missing interval.

### User Story 2 — Compare how fast recipes learn (Priority P1)

The researcher needs to see whether a recipe gains new families, loses familiar cases, or merely spends longer searching.

**Independent test.** Given the same case roster at initialization, 1M and 4M, a reviewer can reproduce both rates, the gains and losses, and the real OEIS row from sealed choices and charges. In a test interval with a six-point gain over 3M useful tokens and 1.5 charged hours, the rates must read +2 points/M and +4 points/hour. A historical eight-attempt score offered as a one-attempt observation fails the compatibility check.

**Acceptance scenario.** Given four gains and four losses on the same cases, when the solve total is unchanged, the report shows all eight transitions and the zero net change.

### User Story 3 — Find out where search loses solutions (Priority P2)

The search researcher can test the fitter with a known structure and then compare search methods that see only the prefix. This work can start while the training wave is being prepared.

**Independent test.** Give the fitter a witnessed structure with constants removed. Include a case with two visible fits that diverge on the suffix, an empty completed bank and a bank containing a correct unselected program. The report distinguishes ambiguity, proposal failure and selection opportunity. Only the prospective method’s sealed choice earns live credit.

**Acceptance scenario.** Given a fixed-order solver that leads D on the same cases, when a learned guide adds no selected solves at matched cost, the decision credits fixed-order search and retains it as the comparator.

### User Story 4 — Spend, resume and decide what the evidence supports (Priority P2)

The research lead needs an affordable next experiment when early results are mixed. The operator must be able to recover the approved work.

**Independent test.** Reverse a ranking by resolving missing cases, and verify the change in spending route. Interrupt a learner update and a staged publication. Two identical resumes must recover one committed outcome, all actual charges and a decision reproducible by the independent reader.

**Acceptance scenario.** Given a promising one-seed result and an unfunded replication, when the screen closes, the report identifies the candidate, comparator and marginal cost, and preserves the sealed replication and assessment panels.

### Edge Cases

- A verified selected solve can coexist with an unfinished fit elsewhere in its portfolio. A completed empty bank is a measured miss. An interrupted empty bank remains unresolved unless the selected outcome is independently established.
- Two problems may share a shifted window or two different programs may fit all visible values. Family membership follows the exposure audit. Ambiguous training labels receive no matching loss.
- A zero-cost receipt reuse, missing historical clock or absent effective shuffle partner produces an explicitly unavailable rate or contrast where required. Shared work appears once in the ledger.
- A missing donor, exhausted mount or shortage of untouched OEIS families names the affected branch and the work that can still proceed.

## Success Criteria

### Measurable Outcomes

- **SC-001** An operator can identify reusable 00 checkpoints and scores, challenged identities, eligible families/pairs, the priced next comparison and explicit shortfalls from preflight alone. Every reused cost and result has one authenticated source.
- **SC-002** All seven registered recipes have either matched 0/1M/4M observations or a recorded disposition and remaining denominator at each missed look. A reviewer can reconstruct both velocity measures, all four transfer strata, retention, paired gains and losses and both factorial interactions. Replacing a one-attempt receipt with an eight-attempt receipt is detected.
- **SC-003** A reviewer can separate known-structure recovery, ambiguous fits, missing proposals, verified passed-over candidates and selected solves. Each live CPU search comparison accounts for all frozen cases and separately shows the gain from the true guide input and the gain from fixed search.
- **SC-004** Decision replays cover a real-OEIS-only gain, a structural-only gain, crossing curves, a reversal under unresolved outcomes, a useful fixed-order solver, and an affordable decision to retain D. Every route names its comparator and marginal cost. A formal gain or replacement claim requires the registered independent panel. Final assessment access remains sealed throughout round029.
- **SC-005** On the candidate workstation a slow valid run and the largest supported input and report survive the entire path. A genuine disposable shortage reports a numeric deficit. The recovered learner matches uninterrupted final weights. Two repeated resumes reproduce one verified report and one committed scientific result, with every discarded attempt still charged.
- **SC-006** An independent reader with the public bundle and pinned reader can reproduce denominators, bounds, uncertainty settings, costs and the next action. Private replay verifies executable choices. A tampered bundle fails verification.
