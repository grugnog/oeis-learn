# Operator, recovery and report contract

This is the proposed round029 interface for development. The current public discovery CLI and historical readers are documented in the [quickstart](../quickstart.md).

## Entry points and authority

Expose `python3 -S -B scripts/foundation_run.py --engine podman discovery round029` from the standard-library host CLI. The candidate launcher is `specs/016-measure-learning-velocity/validation/launch-round029.sh FULL_COMMIT ACTION [AUTHORITY_FILE]`. It verifies the exact Git object, leaves the donor detached, materializes tracked candidate files into an independently verified source cache, and calls the host with distinct source and physical workspace roots. One invocation of `run` drives all eligible operations through independent verification. The identical invocation resumes. Reserve grants by purpose and physical resource. Recording the plan or an expected runtime provides no spending authority.

The authority file is an operator-provided, digest-bound grant with allowed phase, registration/parent, physical owner, maximum spend, follow-up reserve, and eligible branch IDs. Verify the grant against the operator’s independently recorded authorization and bind its immutable contents on first use. A changed grant produces a new authority record. The status reader never launches or reserves. Existing round028 flags, archive types and readers remain usable for their own evidence. Successful negative or inconclusive science is a verified terminal result. A blocked execution returns the original failure and a diagnostic path. Read-only actions succeed when the requested inspection succeeds.

### Wire fields

Use the existing canonical record serializer. The proposed `round029-grant/v1` carries `study_id`, `registration_or_parent_ref`, `scope` (qualification, CPU, exploration or confirmation), `state_root_id`, allowed operation/branch IDs, total and held-back integer microseconds, a resource vector and the authorizing record reference. Verify an unbroken authority chain, nonnegative balances and a follow-up holdback within the grant.

Every machine-readable action result carries `version`, `action`, `session_id`, `mechanical_status`, `scientific_verdict` (nullable), `last_committed_ref`, `remaining_authority`, `next_action` and `reason`. Also return `report_ref` on publication, `verification_scope` on verify, and `first_error` and any measurable `deficit_bytes`/`deficit_inodes` for an affected stage. A missing observation is null with a reason. Typed channel, fit and bank schemas reject additional private payloads in visible records.

Use `BudgetJournal.reserve/commit/reconcile`, cached freeze choices, full-state checkpoints and staged publication. Key 029 operations with typed coordinates. The historical pilot’s string block names and arm/target parsing cannot identify two 4M profiles, seven recipes, two seeds and CPU methods. ## Limits and pricing

An operational guard needs an owner, harm, scope, units, numeric value, source revision, comparable largest/slowest valid case, headroom, exhaustion action and named rehearsal. Audit inherited, new and tightened limits through the final reader. For an essential unmeasured guard, provision twice the larger of the *same-scope* known valid load and projected round029 peak, including concurrent outstanding work. Record the concrete value before it governs a run. If no matching historical observation exists, derive an explicitly provisional peak from the maximum registered workload and actual physical capacity, reserve headroom, then measure it in the named check. Prefer bounded batches or streaming where they remove the need for a guard. Capacity, signed spending authority and scientific opportunity retain their own meanings.

## Publication and independent read

The proposed outputs are `round029-qualification.zip` and `round029-report.zip`, with separate diagnostic output when needed. The independently retained qualification commits the prospective design and eligible scope. For a subsequently authorized child preserve the pre-score commitment for that child and bind the chain to its parent. Validate archive member allowlists and size/nesting/expansion policies at their measured scope. Private replay reopens the original source, scorer, choices, clocks and charges. The archive-only reader receives the [public joint counts](../data-model.md), frozen policy, grant and linked registration digests. It recomputes denominators, paired effects/rates/bounds, costs, scientific verdict and next spending action, including a decision to retain D. Reject a changed count, selector, protected allocation, multiplicity or parent. Scan all exported members and allowed nested encodings with private canaries.
