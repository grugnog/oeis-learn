## Spending and claims

Register the following order, the priced runnable branches and a held-back follow-up allowance before opening comparative scores. Each decision records observation, uncertainty, marginal hours, available authority, alternative uses and the evidence that could change its action.

| Decision point | Action |
| --- | --- |

| Ranking changes under missing-outcome assignments or omission of one influential family | Price the smallest preordered matched bank/fit or family measurement that can change the choice. Use its remaining scientific allowance. If the discriminator is unaffordable, report inconclusive. A sparse direct OEIS signal can earn a reserved independent repeat. |

If none qualifies or can change the decision, retain D and report the limiting measurement. |
