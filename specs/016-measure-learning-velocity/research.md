## 1. Reproduce ordinary at the right boundary

**Decision.** Import compatibility in layers. Preserve the donor’s actual initialization and training-family seeds, ordered positive-row identities, codec, recipe, committed update and measured costs. Within seed one all seven recipes start at the donor’s eligible random initialization. Trial 00 may reuse trained checkpoints. Seed two gets a new paired initialization and independent eligible stream.

**Rationale.** `training_rows.pair_seeds` derives both seeds from the registration digest. Simply registering an identical recipe under round029 changes its initial state. `matched_stream` also binds row order and first-divergence support. The pilot worker assumes two arms, requires those labels for ordinary, fixes logical batch at 32, and picks the full inference profile at 4M. The prior milestones validate charged-token crossings. In round028 ordinary the 4M receipt carries 4,000,210 useful tokens and 2,126 updates. For round029 the milestone is useful exposure, the search profile is explicit at each look, and losses on interrupted work stay in the charge ledger. ## 2. Vary composition supply and input matching independently

**Decision.** Use the deterministic typed program draw and its native and independent execution checks. Fill the ordinary familiar-family slots unchanged. In each remaining training-only length/difficulty band choose the least used eligible canonical composition, breaking ties by frozen seed and draw ID. Keep the new composition statistics and verified pair evidence in private, content-addressed sidecars. Integrate matching with the existing per-program supervised loss and one logical optimizer step.

**Rationale.** The generator exposes structural IDs, the preparation worker checks executed witnesses, and the existing panel machinery supplies composition relations and disjoint calibration. The existing `verified_training_pairs` finds different programs for the same visible input. Round029 needs the same target with different visible inputs. The live decoder leaves explicit parameter programs intact but allows eligible literals in other targets to be abstracted and refitted. A disagreement of one chosen grounding can therefore be an erroneous negative label. ## 3. Reserve linked families and show improvement at comparable costs

**Decision.** Reuse the exposure index and component grouping, with one new round029 allocation policy. Add separate practice/teacher and calibration allocations, retain all earlier reservations and allocate one member per eligible family before second members. Extend the round028 one-attempt probe through 4M. Keep each full-profile endpoint separate.

**Rationale.** The existing exposure index checks all 81 shifted 20-term windows of a 100-term record, source, family and finite behavior. Cross-composition links need the family component step as well. Round028’s allocator has six fixed reservations and may pick two members immediately. Round028’s 0/1M descriptive receipts cannot be joined directly to its eight-attempt full endpoint for a slope. ## 4. Use different estimators for exploration and confirmation

**Decision.** Add a descriptive reducer for linked case transitions, arbitrary signed rates and factorial contrasts. For formal independent confirmation use the existing `metrics.compare` and `decisions.recompute` with explicit rosters and primary strata. A single D choice can participate in both seed contrasts through one outcome and one charge.

**Rationale.** The formal resampler supports a real-OEIS-only endpoint and the existing half-composition/half-unseen endpoint. Its decision bounds assume bounded differences in yields. An interaction, or percentage points per hour, can lie outside that range. Individual method totals cannot reconstruct paired gains/losses, nor can per-look family totals express a shared unresolved choice that cancels in a contrast. `TrialJournal.observe_pilot` and the tuning pruning rule also encode older looks and arms. Add versioned observations without feeding exploratory values to the native sampler. [Formal metrics](../../src/oeis_learn/generalization/progressive/metrics.py) · [Decision reducer](../../src/oeis_learn/generalization/progressive/decisions.py) · [Trials](../../src/oeis_learn/generalization/progressive/trials.py)

**Alternatives considered.** A single score weighted by tokens/second can reward quicker decoding with fewer solves. Comparing linearly interpolated verification dates credits evidence before it was available. At equal cost take each arm’s last verified affordable observation within common support.

## 5. Test structure recovery before choosing a search intervention

**Decision.** Run a privileged structure-only fitter check on disjoint witnessed families. For live search, use one bounded typed frontier with fixed, learned and shuffled action orderings, plus D’s qualified inference path. Learn an action ordering from training-only positive derivations, including verified alternative programs. Privately measure when a correct candidate first entered each sealed bank. Only the submitted choice earns a solve.

**Rationale.** The historical audit found 51 witnessed completed proposal misses and zero confirmed passed-over candidates among 64 structural cases. The incumbent selection ranker scores finished programs. The 028 learner evaluation always loads its model, and forced candidates are marked diagnostic. A separate installed CPU proposer can use the existing fitter, admission and final choice while preserving the live/privileged boundary. Default candidate archives retain representatives and can drop sources. Retrospective coverage needs a priced sample with complete admitted-source and attempt references. [Audit](../015-learned-program-search/validation/round028-audit.md) · [Evaluation](../../src/oeis_learn/pipeline_validation/progressive_evaluation.py) · [Bank inspection](../../src/oeis_learn/generalization/progressive/program_search_bank.py) · [Inference and capture](../../src/oeis_learn/fresh/bigint_diagnostics.py)

**Alternatives considered.** Training a selector on new suffixes would contaminate evaluation. Passing ten alternating values to the current polynomial/recurrence adapters violates their 20-term input contract. A bounded alternating construction can instead track parity and a pair counter in the supported language and ground the combined program against the original prompt. ## 6. Extend the qualified workstation and its readers

**Decision.** Version the graph, worker requests, ledger identities and report reader for 029. Keep one physical state root, session owner and global GPU lease. Authenticate the worker named by a registration even if the repairing controller has a later revision. Budget fit, checkpoint, journal, publication and physical-storage owners separately.

**Rationale.** The eight-hour 028 registration closed before a new score. In the funded child, 10 h 24 min 12 s forecast became 1 h 56 min 56 s charged, including 6 s for selected-choice scoring. Two separate checkpoints were later counted against a journal quota after the report had already been installed. The operators recovered the pending commit without re-export. The existing funding and verification code encodes round028 block names, arms, looks and grants. Keep its identities readable and share its lower-level recovery primitives. [Pilot and timing](../015-learned-program-search/validation/round028-pilot.md) · [Journal recovery](../015-learned-program-search/validation/round028-timing-results.md) · [Funding](../../src/oeis_learn/pipeline_validation/progressive_pilot_funding.py) · [Freeze recovery](../../src/oeis_learn/generalization/progressive/freeze_progress.py)

A timeout for a missing heartbeat can kill legitimate drain or publication.
