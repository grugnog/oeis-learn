# Next-spec handoff · Finding the fastest path to learning OEIS programs

**Proposed round029 research question.** Given one shared workstation, which change most quickly increases the number of new integer sequences for which a learner can propose **one executable program** that predicts all 80 hidden terms from the first 20 indexed terms? Measure progress at equal training exposure and at equal workstation time. The long-term test is transfer to previously unseen real OEIS families. Synthetic problems give us witnesses and faster feedback about *why* performance changes.

Start with round028’s ordinary learner as the training control and D, the older model trained with adaptive allocation of examples, as the operational reference. Screen seven recipes using a common measure of learning velocity. In parallel, use a CPU experiment to estimate how much better search over program structures could buy. Spend the next workstation hours on whichever result points to faster learning, better search, a fitting repair or a missing measurement. Keeping D is a valid outcome. A promising result can earn another experiment well before the evidence is strong enough to replace D.

Before buying another GPU hour, inventory the saved baseline checkpoints, reserve eligible families, and price one matched probe. Run the known-structure fitter check on CPU while preparing the training comparison. Those results determine which measurements are worth paying for first.

This is input for the next Spec Kit **specify** conversation. Round028 is complete. The experiments, looks and spending branches below are proposals for a new registration. The [repository README](../README.md), [constitution](../.specify/memory/constitution.md) and [completed round028 pilot](../specs/015-learned-program-search/validation/round028-pilot.md) supply the background.

## The seven-recipe screen

Use two two-factor comparisons that share a control. The working IDs below belong to this proposal only. All rows use the ordinary positive-program training objective. Three rows vary training data or add an auxiliary exercise. Three vary optimizer settings. This tests main effects and their interaction **within** each group. A cross-group combination would be a separately recorded follow-up.

| Working trial | Mix of training programs | Sequence–program matching exercise | Effective batch | Weight decay | What it buys |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 00 | Round028 ordinary | Off | 32 | 0 | Training control, eligible for historical reuse |
| 01 | Broader compositions | Off | 32 | 0 | Effect of more varied structures |
| 02 | Round028 ordinary | On | 32 | 0 | Effect of learning which prefix belongs with a program |
| 03 | Broader compositions | On | 32 | 0 | Whether the two interventions reinforce each other |
| 04 | Round028 ordinary | Off | 16 | 0 | Batch change alone |
| 05 | Round028 ordinary | Off | 32 | 0.01 | Decay change alone |
| 06 | Round028 ordinary | Off | 16 | 0.01 | Both optimizer changes together |

**Broader compositions.** Generate more distinct combinations of already-supported primitives per million training tokens. Independently execute their witnesses and retain familiar training families. Specify how sampling changes, including the distribution of lengths, difficulty and family frequencies on both streams. Use only eligible, mechanically generated training families. Changing the held-out roster or adding a named OEIS-family teacher would answer a different question. [Exposure and panel design](../specs/015-learned-program-search/contracts/experiment.md#families-difficulty-and-access)

**Matching exercise.** Pair a verified training program with its true prefix and a superficially similar training prefix for which it is demonstrably wrong. Reward the model for preferring the correct pairing using the visible input. If both examples map to the same parameterized decoder target, changing the constants may let the same structure fit both. Check the representation and legal grounding actually used in training. Exclude ambiguous or unfinished negatives and report how much training support remains. Choose the exercise weight on training-only data, then freeze it. Report extra passes, compute and eligible examples. This tests whether the model uses its input when proposing a whole program. **Matched baseline and reuse.** Start the recipes for one seed from identical eligible random weights. Hold the architecture, grammar, fitter, visible-only choice policy, precision, constant baseline learning rate (multiplier 1) and zero dropout fixed. Share the same ordered positive rows wherever the tested factor permits. Record the change in optimizer updates per million tokens when batch size changes. Verify donor weights, optimizer/RNG/data position for continuation, row stream, source/profile, costs and exposure. Reuse a saved score only on matching prompts, search profile, policy and scoring rule. If only the probe changes, re-evaluate the saved weights. If training provenance cannot support a matched comparison, rerun only the affected control. A later branch from a 4M parent measures continued learning. ## Follow the most informative result

The default is a **4M endpoint for each viable recipe**, with 1M as a diagnostic. With reusable trial 00 this amounts to about 24M new useful tokens before whole-update overshoot, replication or continuation. Price the actual wave on the workstation. Reserve capacity for a follow-up before spending it all on initial arms. The seven recipes, 1M/4M looks and possible 8M continuation are planning choices. Agree on a different affordable scope prospectively if required. A one-seed shortlist can guide spending without a formal gain verdict. Apply the old 95% completion rule to its formal claims. A sound exploratory run can earn its next look even if completion at 1M is lower.

| What we observe | Where the next unit of work goes |
| --- | --- |
| A recipe gains exact solves on the reserved OEIS development families at comparable cost, or produces a credible structural gain while OEIS evidence is sparse | Shortlist using paired gains/losses, missing-outcome bounds, cost, conditioning controls and retention. A direct OEIS signal merits its own repeat even if the structural proxy is flat. Label a structural-only gain as a transfer hypothesis. |
| Recipes trade speed for quality, or the order changes between 1M and 4M | Show the trade-off. Preserve a plausible late learner. If more exposure would distinguish learning curves, continue **both** the finalist and ordinary from their full states to a registered longer look, initially proposed at 8M. Bind the extra data and learning-rate schedule and retain prior charges. |
| An apparent gain depends on which problems finished, one prolific family, or slow/empty search | Spend on the smallest matched measurement that could change the choice. Include every frozen case. Show favorable and adverse assignments of unresolved results when they could change the ranking. Record an inconclusive outcome when the missing information cannot justify its cost. |
| Search regularly fails to propose a usable structure, or correct programs appear and then lose selection | Use the CPU experiment below to test structure recovery and search. Pursue selection only where verified alternatives were actually passed over. If the fitter regularly fails with a known structure, isolate that failure before paying for more decoder training. |
| Fixed-order search improves yield and the learned guide adds little, or shuffling its input preserves the gain | Credit the search improvement. Use that solver as a comparator and test what input information could make a learned guide useful. |
| A candidate is worth trusting beyond the tuning set | Compare finalist and ordinary under a new paired training seed on untouched families, retain D as the operational reference and measure all on the common replication panel. Include the first seed on the same panel if making a two-seed claim. Predeclare retention, completion, uncertainty and multiplicity for adoption. A gain over ordinary is evidence about training. Replacing D requires a direct replicated comparison. |
| Nothing improves the attainable exact yield for a worthwhile cost | Keep D. Publish the comparisons and identify the limiting factor or missing evidence. Larger models, 25M training and final assessment each require a new reason and appropriate authority. |

Freeze the initial comparisons, budget and allowed decision branches before opening their scores. At each spending decision record the observation, uncertainty, estimated **marginal** cost, what result could change the decision and why this branch is the most useful one. If a new idea emerges from an exposed result, give it a new registration and independent evaluation. This lets the study follow evidence without retroactively changing the rules of an earlier trial. ### Parallel CPU experiment on search

Use separate synthetic practice families with known executable witnesses. First supply **only the true structure** with its constants removed and let the existing fitter recover the continuation from the 20 visible terms. Measure how often that succeeds and how often different plausible programs agree on those 20 and diverge later. This is a privileged diagnostic available on synthetic tasks. Its solves cannot enter the live leaderboard.

Next build a fixed-order search over legal operations, reusing the existing grammar, templates and fitter. For example, a search can split alternating terms and try a simple rule on each part, then compile the result back into the supported language. On training families only, collect verified construction steps and teach a small guide which operation to try next from the prefix and the partially executed program. Accept independently verified alternative solutions as training evidence where eligible. Include a fixed-order control, the same learned guide with shuffled input, and D with its own normal inference policy. All live methods receive only permitted data and submit one choice. Compare them on identical cases and matched end-to-end search allowances. Charge internal expansions, fits, model calls, elapsed time and any offline teacher work in the appropriate ledger. Report whether the guide finds correct candidates earlier, whether it improves final selected yield, and what seeing the real sequence contributes. Schedule this alongside training only where resource contention will not distort the measured comparison. Record its effect on search separately from the training curves. The CPU prototype can proceed independently of the screening wave.
